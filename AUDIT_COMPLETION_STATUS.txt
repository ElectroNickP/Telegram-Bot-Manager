🛠️ WHAT WAS FIXED (CRITICAL)

✅ 1. Hardcoded Flask Secret Key
   Before: app.secret_key = "your-secret-key-change-in-production"
   After:  Loaded from FLASK_SECRET_KEY environment variable
   Impact: Session hijacking vulnerability ELIMINATED

✅ 2. Insecure Session Cookies
   Before: SESSION_COOKIE_SECURE=False (always)
   After:  SESSION_COOKIE_SECURE=is_production (auto-enabled)
   Impact: MITM attack vector CLOSED

✅ 3. Default Credentials Hardcoded
   Before: SHA256 hash of 'admin' hardcoded
   After:  Require ADMIN_PASSWORD_HASH from .env
   Impact: Known default credentials ELIMINATED
